# Privacy Policy for Ezan Vakti Premium

**Last Updated:** August 7, 2026

Ezan Vakti Premium ("the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled when using our application.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use

### a. Location Data
The App requests access to your device's location (GPS / Network) solely to compute accurate Islamic Prayer Times (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) and the exact Qibla direction towards the Kaaba in Mecca.
- **Local Processing Only:** Location data is processed strictly on your device.
- **No Remote Transmission:** Location data is **never transmitted to external servers, stored remotely, tracked, or shared with third parties.**

### b. Device Sensors
Device magnetometer and accelerometer sensors are read in real-time on the Qibla Compass screen to point towards Mecca. Sensor readings are processed locally and never recorded or transmitted.

### c. Local Data Storage
User preferences, prayer tracking logs, zikr counts, bookmarked verses, and theme settings are saved locally on your device using `SharedPreferences`. This data remains on your device and is removed when uninstalling the App.

---

## 2. Notifications & Adhan Alarms
Local notifications are scheduled on your device to trigger Adhan alarms at prayer times. Notifications operate offline without transmitting any data to remote servers.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services
The App fetches Holy Quran text and translations via public APIs (AlQuran Cloud API, Fawaz Ahmed Quran CDN). No personally identifiable information (PII) or device identifiers are transmitted during these requests.

---

## 4. Children's Privacy
The App does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 years of age.
