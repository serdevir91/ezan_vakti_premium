Privacy Policy / Gizlilik Politikası

Last Updated: August 7, 2026

Summary: Ezan Vakti Premium does not collect, sell, or transmit any personal user data to external servers. Your location and sensor data are processed strictly locally on your device to calculate accurate prayer times and Qibla direction.

1. Data Collection and Usage

Ezan Vakti Premium ("the App") respects your privacy. Below is an explanation of data used to deliver core application features:

a. Location Data

The App requests access to your device's fine/coarse location (GPS/Network) solely to compute accurate Islamic Prayer Times (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) and exact Qibla direction towards Mecca.

  • Location data is processed locally on your device only.
  • Location information is never transmitted to external servers, stored remotely, tracked, or shared with third parties.

b. Magnetometer & Compass Sensors

Device magnetometer and accelerometer sensors are read in real-time on the Qibla Compass screen to point towards the Kaaba. Sensor readings are never stored or uploaded.

c. Local Storage

User preferences (prayer completion status, zikr counts, bookmark notes, theme choices) are stored locally on your device via SharedPreferences. This data remains on your device and is erased when uninstalling the App.

2. Local Notifications & Adhan Alarms

Local notifications are scheduled on-device to trigger Adhan alarms at prayer times. Notifications operate offline and do not rely on remote tracking or external push servers.

3. Third-Party Services

The App fetches Quran text and translations via public APIs (AlQuran Cloud API, Fawaz Ahmed Quran CDN). No personally identifiable user data is sent with these requests.

4. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under 13. All features are designed for safe use by all age groups.
